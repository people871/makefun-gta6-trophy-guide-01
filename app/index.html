    Platinum roadmap — the order to earn them in

    Six stages, built from the official descriptions. Story first, then stranger strands, then activities, collectibles, robberies and money, and the long grinds last. Save manually at the start of every chapter: the game has no mission replay (OutputLag).

    ⚠ Missable flag — 1 trophy: Positive Mental Attitude (#28). Meditate at every spot you find, starting in Chapter 1 — one spot disappears after that chapter and there is no mission replay (OutputLag / KeenGamer). Every other trophy can be finished in free roam after the story.

      Before you start

      • Manual saves: keep one at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission — there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus (OutputLag).
      • Cheats off: PowerPyx reports that turning on a cheat disables trophies (via PlanetGTA6).
      • One missable: Positive Mental Attitude is the only potentially missable trophy (KeenGamer). Meditate at every spot you find, starting in Chapter 1.
      • Time: about 200 hours for the platinum is the early estimate (OutputLag). No online trophies and no difficulty trophies — it is long, not hard.
      • 100% last: A Long Road (gold, 100% completion) is the hardest trophy (KeenGamer) and comes last, once every other stage is done.

      Missable warnings

      Only one trophy is confirmed as potentially missable. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story — but with no mission replay, manual saves are still your safety net.

      Save discipline (from the official descriptions + OutputLag)

      • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.
      • Keep a separate save before the final mission.
      • Meditate at every meditation spot the moment you find it — do not leave them for after the story.
