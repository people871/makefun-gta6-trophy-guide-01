      About Leonida Platinum

      This is a single-page companion for hunting all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies in Grand Theft Auto VI: one platinum, two gold, six silver, and forty-six bronze. Every trophy keeps its official name and description, plus a guide written in plain words that cites the site each fact came from.

      How the citation count is made: 282 of 282 claims cited. Each trophy contributes three official facts — its name, description and grade, all verified against Exophase — for 165 claims. On top of that, every guide is written as numbered steps, and each of the 117 steps carries its own source citation. A step with no source behind it is never written, and a trophy whose unlock method is still unconfirmed at launch says so instead of guessing. Counts that could not be verified are left out rather than invented.

      Tick a trophy off and it stays ticked — your progress lives only in this browser, on this device, and is never sent anywhere. You can save a backup as a JSON file, load one back in, or wipe the slate clean and start over.

      Hidden trophies stay masked until you earn them, so browsing the list will not spoil story moments for you. The spoiler shield button in the filter bar turns that masking on and off, and the setting is remembered. The roadmap view orders the hunt into six stages, and the missable view calls out the one trophy that can be locked out if you rush past the first chapter.

      The whole app runs from files on your own machine. There are no accounts, no ads, and no network requests: open the page, check things off, and close it when you are done.

      Progress is stored locally in your browser. Export keeps a backup copy as a file.
